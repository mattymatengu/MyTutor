How it works

Three steps to better marks

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Register as a student with your NUST email. Tutors register too and wait for approval.

  2. 2

    Apply for a module

    Pick a module, choose a tutor (or let any approved tutor accept) and tell them what you need.

  3. 3

    Learn and review

    Join sessions, use the materials and rate your tutor to help other students.
