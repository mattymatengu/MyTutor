About MyTutor

Students helping students pass

MyTutor is a campus service built for the Namibia University of Science and Technology (NUST).

The problem we solve

Many NUST students struggle with difficult modules but do not know who can help. Notes are scattered across WhatsApp groups, and good tutors are hard to find.

MyTutor gives every module one home: approved tutors, shared materials and a calendar of live sessions.

What you can do

  • Browse the module catalogue and search by code, name, department or level.
  • Apply for tutoring and track the status of your application.
  • Download notes, slides and past papers shared by tutors.
  • RSVP to live tutoring sessions and rate your tutor afterwards.

Frequently asked questions

Is MyTutor free?

Yes. Students do not pay to use the platform.

How do I become a tutor?

Register as a tutor, describe your qualification and choose the modules you want to teach. An administrator reviews your application before you can log in.

Who can see the materials?

Tutors choose whether a file is public to all logged-in students or only for the students they tutor.
